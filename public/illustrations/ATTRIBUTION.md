Prompts for the original 2026-10-15 illustrations: blue-ink and pale watercolor illustrations of a Vietnamese window-side workspace and a family overlooking a riverside city. No third-party product logos or assets.

Office marks in `public/brand/office/*-new.png` and `office-mark.png` come from the owner's supplied `PI office … tach nen` PNGs; their excess canvas was cropped and their exterior white background cleared, then resized for web delivery. Web identity icons, tool file-handler icons and social cards are derived from these exact marks by `npm run brand:assets`. No marks are redrawn by AI.

The current landing hero uses `OfficePreview.tsx`, a code-rendered laptop illustration with these same Office marks. It follows the landing's light/dark theme and labels sample documents as illustrative. The retired raster laptop mockups had inconsistent embedded tool marks and are no longer shipped in `public/`.
